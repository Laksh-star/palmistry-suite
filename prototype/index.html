Before we begin

A reading describes what two traditions of palmistry have said about the shapes they see in a hand. Palmistry has not been shown scientifically to predict anything, so treat this as a reflection, not a forecast.

Two traditions, read separately

Each tradition uses only its own sourced rules. Where a tradition has nothing to say about what was seen, it says so rather than borrowing from the other.

Putting them side by side

The synthesis shows where the traditions agree, where they differ and where only one speaks. Agreement between two traditions does not make a reading more likely to be true.

Ask about your reading

Answers come only from this session's evidence. If the evidence doesn't cover a question, or the question is out of scope, the answer says so.
